# Brand: FLMLNK

Sources: logo files Dw uploaded (Nov 26) and the public site www.flmlnk.com (read Nov 26, 2026). Blank fields come from Dw, never invented.

## Identity
- One-line positioning (site hero sub): "We turn buried film catalogs back into earning assets."
- Hero headline: "Old films. New *believers.*"
- Category language on site: "audience underwriting and catalog recovery"
- Audience (site): owners of film catalogs (distributors, rights holders): "Let's talk about your catalog." Meta description still says "Marketing engine for filmmakers and their films" (older positioning, also on the old social card).
- Mood words: cinematic, warm-dark, confident, evidence-led, quiet
- Feels next to: prestige film marketing, A24-style restraint. Never: hype-tech neon, influencer/creator-tool energy.

## Type
- Display / UI: **DM Sans**, weight 450 to 500, tight, sentence case
- Accent: **Instrument Serif Italic** for one emphasised word or phrase per headline, in gold `#D3B58B`
- Both are Google Fonts (OFL): install via `@fontsource/dm-sans` and `@fontsource/instrument-serif` at build time
- Pattern: two-line headline, line 2 starts with or ends on the serif italic word. Period at the end.

## Voice (from site copy)
- Short declaratives, often paired: "Not every film in a catalog deserves another dollar. A few clearly do."
- Contrast pairs: "Demand Before Spend", "Evidence, Not Forecasts", "Projected / Measured"
- Words it uses: catalog, titles, audience, evidence, back, earn, discover, deserve, buried, overlooked
- Never: hype, guarantees, "content", "followers", forecasts presented as fact
- VO: none by default (style carries it on cards + music)
