## Signature moves (ranked)

1. **Card / product alternation.** 11 white text cards in 58 s, each 3 to 7 words over two centred lines, ~2 s, always between product beats. One idea per card.
2. **Word-by-word type builds.** Lines appear a word or phrase at a time on the beat, never a whole-line fade.
3. **Hero object on seamless white, moving by itself.** Products rotate, tilt and fan into place; colourways shown side by side. No set, no props, no shadows beyond a soft contact shadow.
4. **One hand is the only human in the room.** People appear only inside screens; a single hand enters to tap, swipe or hand off.
5. **Carousel finale.** A continuous lateral push where each item shoves the last off frame, landing on the hero.
6. **One colour break.** A single macro of the coloured material is the only saturated moment.

## Look, type, sound

- Ground: off-white `#f1f1f3`, almost no saturation (0.07), neutral warmth. Colour lives only in the product and its screens.
- Light: soft, top-front studio light, contact shadows only, no visible source.
- Type: black, medium-weight sans, small (about 4% of frame height), centred, generous leading. Legal lines tiny under the line.
- Sound: light upbeat electronic at ~126 BPM, -15.7 LUFS, no voiceover; type builds land on beats.

## Translate, never copy

Their wordmark, their logo morph, their UI, the watch-face wall, their exact lines and their music stay with them. What transfers: the card/product rhythm, word builds, the white seamless world, the lone hand, the carousel finale, one colour break.
